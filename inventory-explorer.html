THE PROGRAM IS SIMPLE - EVERYTHING SAVES AUTOMATICALLY

  • Click New and enter information to create an item.
  • Click New in the panel to use the current location.
  • Click Import to copy an item to your lab.

SAFETY DATA SHEETS

  • Place SDS sheets in a Google Drive folder to link to them.
  • Search existing SDS sheets and link or copy to your folder.

BARCODE SCANNERS

  • Click the ID Field and scan to enter a barcode.
  • Click the Search Field and scan to find by barcode.

LOCATION ALLOWS YOU TO SEARCH FOR ITEMS BY WHERE THEY ARE

(DSC 122 Fridge Shelf 2 PCR Box).

  • Click Location 1 or Location 2 to view nearby items.
  • Place items in Boxes to collect them as a List or Grid.
  • Click Use to remove an item and decrease the Stock.
  • Click Move then Archive to archive items temporarily. Accidentally deleted items can be recovered from the backup.

ORDERING TRACKS ORDERS AND ARRIVALS SO YOU CAN RECEIVE ITEMS

How to Order an Item:

  1. When you order an item, it moves to the top of the main list.
    Click OrderStatus changes to On Order
  2. When an item arrives, your team can update the stock.
    Click StockStatus changes to Arrived
  3. Receive the item in your procurement system.
    Click InputStatus changes to In Stock
  • Set a Threshold to determine when an item is Low Stock.

SHARING TRACKS ITEMS SO YOU CAN SEE WHO BORROWED WHAT AND WHEN

Set a Policy to Control Your Items:

  • NoOthers can’t borrow the item.
  • AskOthers must send a request to borrow the item.
  • YesTracks name and date so you know where items are.
  • Click Recall to request return of a shared item.
  • Click Accept or Deny to control restricted items.
  • Click Resolve to clear outstanding debts.

How to Borrow an Item:

  1. Toggle the main Search Bar to All.
  2. Search for the item you need.
  3. Enter your name, the date, and click Borrow or Request.
  • Click Return to give an item back.
  • Click IOU if you owe a replacement.
